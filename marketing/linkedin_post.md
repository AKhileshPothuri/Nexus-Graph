## 🚀 I just launched Nexus-Graph: The Intelligence Map of your Codebase.

A few weeks ago, I almost uninstalled my AI coding assistant. 

The reason? I realized I was paying a "Noise Tax." My AI was pulling entire 500-line files just to understand a single 10-line function. It was inefficient, expensive, and frankly, it made the AI dumber.

I decided to fix it. 

I built **Nexus-Graph**—a local-first retrieval engine that treats your code like a living symbol graph, not just a collection of text files. 

### Why this matters:
🔹 **Surgical Precision**: It uses BFS traversal to find only the specific functions and dependencies your AI needs. No more "context junk."
🔹 **70% Token Savings**: By cutting out the boilerplate, you aren't just saving money—you're getting more accurate results from the LLM.
🔹 **Local-First Privacy**: Your code graph never leaves your machine. 

Building this has totally changed how I work with AI. It’s no longer about "how much context can I cram in?" but "how precise can the context be?"

We’re officially live on NPM today!
